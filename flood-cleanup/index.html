In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Pearland Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Pearland to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

How to Tell You Need Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
